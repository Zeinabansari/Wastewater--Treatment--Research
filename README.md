# Wastewater Treatment Research
## Comparative Assessment of Advanced Water and Wastewater Treatment Technologies: Treatment Performance, Energy Consumption, and Operational Limitations
### Project Overview
This project presents a comparative literature-based assessment of advanced water and wastewater treatment technologies, based on 11 selected research papers.

The study focuses on three main aspects:

Treatment performance
Energy consumption
Operational limitations
Technologies Covered
Membrane-based treatment processes
Advanced oxidation processes (AOPs)
Plasma-based treatment technologies
Other related treatment approaches
### Research Objectives
The main objective is to examine how different treatment technologies perform under different operating conditions and to identify their energy requirements, operational constraints, and sustainability considerations.
###
### Methodology
Information was extracted and organized from 11 selected research papers. The dataset includes treatment technologies, wastewater types, target pollutants, reported treatment performance, operating conditions, energy indicators, and operational limitations.

### Key Considerations
Reported performance and energy indicators are not always directly comparable across studies. Differences in wastewater characteristics, target pollutants, treatment configurations, experimental scales, and reporting methods must be considered.

Therefore, this project avoids misleading cross-study averages and unsupported overall technology rankings.

### Scope and Limitations
This is a comparative literature assessment based on 11 selected studies. It is not a complete systematic review or an experimental investigation. The findings are limited by the information and indicators reported in the selected papers.

The final research report, comparative dataset, figures, and reference list will be provided in this repository.
